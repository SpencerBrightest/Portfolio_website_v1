<Block title="How I learn">
When I started Flutter, I was new to almost every concept and my code broke at every step. It was frustrating, but I noticed that whenever it broke, I could hand the error to my code agent and get it fixed. Each fix showed me what I'd gotten wrong, and over time the breaks got fewer. I learn through real projects like this: I build something, let it break, and treat every fix as a lesson. Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT are my tutors along the way.

I don't just accept the fix — I ask why it broke and make sure I understand the underlying pattern. That keeps me learning and fits right into my core philosophy of *Learning to Build With AI Without Losing the Thread*.
</Block>
