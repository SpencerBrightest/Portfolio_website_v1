On 30 May 2026, I took part in the NAHPI Hackathon at the NAHPI complex. I went in with TalentBridge CM, a bilingual (English and French) AI-powered job-matching platform for Cameroon.

## The idea

Finding a job in Cameroon is hard for a lot of reasons, and language is one of them. TalentBridge matches job seekers to jobs with a weighted score: skills count for 50%, experience 25%, education 15% and language 10%. It also includes an AI CV builder and Orange Money integration, so it fits how people here actually pay.

## Preparing

Before the hackathon, I put together the pitch deck, wrote the SRS documentation, and worked on the logo and the overall look of the product. Putting in the hours before event day ensured our team was completely aligned on the vision and ready for presentation.

## The day

Demonstrating TalentBridge CM live at the NAHPI complex was an intense and thrilling experience. Pitching a bilingual platform tailored specifically to the Cameroonian job market resonated deeply during the live demo and Q&A session with the judges.
I wasn't  amongst the best three but i enjoyed all the moments of the competition
## What I learned

Before the hackathon, I thought of myself as someone who builds projects. Pitching TalentBridge changed that. A project is only worth something if it solves a real problem for real people, and if it can make money, because that's what lets it last. A pitch is how you prove all of that quickly: who has the problem, how your product fixes it, and how it earns. I learned to explain the problem first and the features second. Building something that works isn't enough. It has to matter to someone enough that they'd pay for it.
